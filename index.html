Welcome to Centre for Net Zero Technologies (CNET)

The Centre for Net-Zero Technologies (CNET) is a multi-disciplinary research and innovation centre focused on developing advanced, sustainable, and scalable technologies to support India’s transition towards a carbon-neutral future. CNET focuses on mission-oriented research, technology development, innovation, and industry-academia collaboration in strategically important areas of the net-zero ecosystem. The Centre aims to bridge the gap between fundamental research and real-world implementation.

CNET’s thrust areas span the entire technology chain, from clean energy generation and storage to sustainable transportation, resource recovery, carbon reduction, and climate-resilient solutions. CNET is committed to nurturing researchers, innovators, and technology entrepreneurs while contributing to national and global sustainability goals. The Centre envisions becoming a leading platform for research excellence, technological innovation, capacity building, and translational research that supports India’s journey towards a net-zero, sustainable future.
